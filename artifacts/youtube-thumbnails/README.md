## Shared art direction

- Premium B2B Voice AI and analytics aesthetic.
- Voker palette: deep teal `#004D43`, hover teal `#003D36`, pale mint `#DDEAED`, white, and slate `#0F172A`.
- Restrained analytics accents: positive green `#0F9D7A`, info blue `#3B82F6`, and diagnostic coral `#EF5A67`.
- Bold modern sans-serif headlines, strong thumbnail-scale contrast, generous safe margins, and no small body copy.
