# 60–90 second demo flow

1. **Voice Impact Overview**
   - Start on the KPI row and the “Top Voice Issues by Impact” panel.
   - Wow line: “Traditional voice observability tells you interruptions happened. This shows whether those interruptions are associated with resolution.”

2. **Open `Reschedule Appointment`**
   - Show the healthy vs problematic cohorts.
   - Highlight: calls with >3 interruptions resolve much less often than calls with low interruption rate.
   - This is the primary wow moment.

3. **Open a failed example call**
   - Play a short excerpt.
   - Show transcript correction, slow finalization, interruption count, and incorrect downstream action.
   - Evidence turns the chart into a believable story.

4. **Optional: Benchmark STT providers**
   - Replay the failed calls against candidate providers.
   - Compare WER, critical entity accuracy, p90 finalization, and intent recovery.
   - Position this as a follow-up diagnostic, not the main product.

## Core product statement
“Voker already knows whether users resolve their intent. Voice Impact connects technical voice behavior to those outcomes and surfaces the patterns most associated with failure.”
